# Cyber Quest

Stay safe in your digital home now that AI can attack. Two friends play the game: **Ying** (林影), an ethical hacker, tries to sneak in; **Philia** defends your home with you.

- ~20 questions → a personal plan of "doors" to close, with step-by-step fixes for your phone, computer, router and accounts
- Fix mode: ✓ done / ✗ didn't work / ? stuck (gives a prompt for any AI chat)
- Spot the trick: practise on real-world scam messages
- Quest log saved on the phone; copy or email it to yourself
- AI help without anyone paying for anyone else: a copyable briefing for your own AI, or your own OpenRouter key (stays on your phone)
- 🆘 card for "I think I've been hacked"

Static, offline PWA, EN/DE, no accounts, no tracking, no outside requests (except openrouter.ai if you enter your own key). Art made with Gemini. MIT licence.

Status: prototype, Nov 2026.
